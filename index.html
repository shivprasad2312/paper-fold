How to play

  1. 1
    Draw a path. Press and drag anywhere in the level to sketch a solid line. Lift your pencil and the tap opens — that is when gravity starts. You can keep drawing afterwards: every extra line goes into the same running world.
  2. 2
    Fill the glass. Guide the falling water into the glass until it reaches the dotted line. Every glass must be filled.
  3. 3
    Save your ink. The green meter is your ink. Stay in the first block for 3 stars, the second for 2, the last for 1. Tap the round arrow to try again.
  4. 4
    Hold that glass. Some glasses hang in mid-air and fall as soon as things move. Sketch a shelf under one and it will sit still while you pour.
  5. 5
    Anchor your ink. Ink is heavy: a line that isn't hooked onto something drops out of the level. Start the line just under the tap, on a plank, a crate or a push-pin and it holds there. Hook only one end and it swings from that point and settles.
  6. 6
    Use the scenery. You can't draw on planks, crates, spinners or inside a glass — but all of them make great anchors and walls. Push-pins, blowers, diamonds and heated pads are all fair game.

Tips

  • Aim a line at the centre of the stream instead of trying to catch every drop.
  • Unanchored lines fall, so peg long ramps to two pins or a wall whenever you can.
  • Short hooks and wedges beat long bridges — and cost less ink.
  • Moving hazards waste water, so draw a shield before the stream reaches them.
